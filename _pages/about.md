I take regulated, high-stakes AI from promising pilot to measurable, controlled and audit-ready production.

I have more than 10 years of experience designing, deploying and scaling AI systems used by more than 500 million people, in startups and in enterprises. My work spans technical leadership, deep ML expertise, team management and mentoring, and full ownership of projects across their whole lifecycle.

I also write about how LLMs and AI agents work in practice. My 2023 post on [the tricks behind 100K context windows](https://blog.gopenai.com/how-to-speed-up-llms-and-use-100k-context-window-all-tricks-in-one-place-ffd40577b4c) collected the techniques that make long context possible. My latest study measures [how often coding agents pick the right skill or MCP tool](https://galinaalperovich.github.io/in-context-forgetting/) as more of them are installed, with all the data and code [open on GitHub](https://github.com/galinaalperovich/in-context-forgetting).
